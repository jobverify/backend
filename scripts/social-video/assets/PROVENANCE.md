# Media provenance

JobVerify logo: existing application-owned `jobverify-frontend/public/jobverify-logo.png`; included for this application's branded videos.

Noto Sans Bold: `NotoSans-Bold.ttf`, downloaded from the official [notofonts/noto-fonts repository](https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts/blob/main/hinted/ttf/NotoSans/NotoSans-Bold.ttf) on 2026-11-20. Covered by the bundled `OFL-NotoSans.txt` license alongside the regular font.

Employer icons: fetched at render time from the job's explicit official employer domain. These company-owned marks are used to identify the employer of the listed vacancy. Original source URLs and byte hashes appear in each render manifest; no employer marks are bundled in this source directory. Unknown/unavailable brands render initials.

Noto Sans Regular: downloaded from the official [Noto font repository](https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts/blob/main/hinted/ttf/NotoSans/NotoSans-Regular.ttf). Distributed under SIL OFL 1.1, copied alongside the font. Font software remains under its original license.

Instrumental: original computer-synthesized composition in `../compose_music.py`, generated on 2026-11-20. No recordings, samples, or third-party music. The generated music is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The application mixes it at 12% gain after a restrained source amplitude, with entrance/exit fades.

Narration: generated at rendering time using edge-tts and the configured Indian English voice. It is not a cloned voice. No narration recording is committed as a production asset.

Anton Regular: `Anton-Regular.ttf`, downloaded from the official [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/anton/Anton-Regular.ttf) on 2026-11-20. Distributed under the bundled `OFL-Anton.txt` SIL Open Font License. Used for burned captions.

QR artwork: generated locally from each API-owned JobVerify job URL using qrcode 8.2; no external QR generator or redirect service is used.
